Fortale is an AI-powered creative book studio that turns your ideas into illustrated, audible, and shareable digital books. Start with a topic, character, or question to build a fairy tale, story, or workbook in a few steps. Fortale helps transform that initial spark into a complete personal book experience, from cover to chapters, and reading to listening.

Build your own story world by choosing the age group, genre, sub-genre, theme, narrative tone, time, and setting. Describe your characters and core idea to shape the language, rhythm, and atmosphere. Whether creating a warm bedtime story, a long narrative with deepening worlds, or a structured workbook for learning, your prompts define the final result.

You set the direction in the creation flow. Elaborate on a short idea, describe your own sub-genre and unique world, and define basic information for multiple characters. As the book progresses, the cover, chapters, and narrative world are shaped around your vision. Re-read the work or continue creating until you capture the perfect feel for your story.

Add an optional portrait to make your character more personal. This creative reference helps visualize the main character consistently, allowing you to build a familiar connection between the cover, chapter illustrations, and the story world.

Fortale extends your book experience beyond text. See the story with cover and chapter illustrations, and listen with page narration, podcasts, and background audio. Switch between reading and listening as you wish, continuing your story while traveling, resting, or whenever you want to share it.

Every book has its own path. Highlight imagination in fairy tales, deepen character choices in stories, or explore concepts in workbooks. Fortale offers a flexible space to produce books in different genres and for various reading purposes from a single idea.

Fortale provides a powerful starting point for learning. When creating a workbook, choose your level to establish an organized flow with explanations, examples, reinforcement sections, quizzes, and related book suggestions. Progress according to educational needs from primary school to university, reading and listening at your own pace.

Your books stay in your personal library. Search, filter, and organize your creations, keeping access smooth with device caching. Download books as PDFs to read, print, or share, or export them as EPUB files for your e-book reader.

Discover new stories in the Fortale community, publish your books, and share your work. Browse various genres for inspiration and grow your library with books you love. Access your published works or saved books directly from the reading flow.
