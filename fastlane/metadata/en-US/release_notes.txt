Fortale 1.0.4 makes personal and exciting book production more powerful. The focus of this version is the portrait-supported production experience, which transforms the character of your dreams into a more familiar, consistent, and impressive narrative. With the optional portrait addition flow, you can include your main character in the book in a more visually stable way, helping the character appear more integrated in the cover, chapter visuals, and scene atmosphere while creating tales, stories, and novels. The portrait is a creative reference used to depict the main character of the book; the goal is to create a more recognizable and personal book feeling in every chapter. Book production quality has been significantly improved in this version. The selected genre, sub-genre, age group, narrative tone, and creative prompts are now reflected in the text more consistently. A warmer, curiosity-inducing, and child-appropriate flow in tales; clearer scene transitions, stronger character motivation, and more vivid atmosphere in stories; and longer-lasting plot, more balanced chapter structure, and more immersive tension in novels were targeted. Thus, Fortale focuses on creating an enjoyable and high-excitement book experience rather than just producing text from a short idea. Visual integrity has also been strengthened. The production flow has been organized so that the link between the cover visual, chapter visuals, and character narration is established more carefully. Especially in portrait-supported books, it is aimed to achieve a more natural harmony between the character's appearance, the story world, and the scene tone. Users' tale, story, and novel ideas approach a more ready-to-publish form with cover, chapter visuals, narrative rhythm, and exportable book output. Stability improvements have also been made in the production and reading experience. Processing of large book packages has been made more reliable, local caching of cover and book content has been strengthened, and waiting and timeout issues that may occur during book openings have been reduced. While the reading flow on mobile screens is kept cleaner, text size and layout balance have been improved on iPad and wide screens. The general visual language has also been made more readable, modern, and more compatible with the new book studio feeling of the application. This version also maintains the openness that protects user trust. The purpose of the image uploaded in the portrait-supported production flow is to help consistently depict the main character of the relevant book. Produced book visuals and book outputs can be saved, but the principle that the portrait reference is not used as a permanent profile picture, advertising asset, or separate media archive is maintained. In general, 1.0.4 is a development version focusing on portrait-supported personalization, higher quality and more exciting book production, stronger cover and chapter visual integrity, more reliable book opening, and a more consistent reading experience. We continue to refine the core mechanics of Fortale to ensure that every story you craft is as unique as your imagination. By enhancing the way our engine interprets your creative prompts, we have ensured that the emotional depth of your characters remains consistent from the first page to the last.
