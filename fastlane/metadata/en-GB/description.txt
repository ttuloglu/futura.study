Fortale is an AI-powered creative book studio designed to transform your ideas into illustrated, audible, and shareable digital books. Start with a topic, character, or intriguing question; create your tale, story, or workbook in a few steps. Fortale helps you turn that initial spark into a personal book experience, from cover to chapters, reading to listening.

Build your own story world. Choose the age group, genre, sub-genre, theme, narrative tone, time, and setting; describe your characters and the core idea of the story. Whether it is a warm bedtime story for your child, a long story with deepening characters, or a structured workbook, let your prompts shape the language, rhythm, and atmosphere of the book.

You set the direction in the creation flow. You can elaborate on a short idea, describe your own sub-genre and unique world, and define basic information for multiple characters. As the book progresses, the cover, chapters, and narrative world are shaped around the same idea. You can open and re-read the resulting work; continue creating with new ideas until you capture the feeling of the story.

You can add an optional portrait to make your character more personal. The portrait is a creative reference that helps visually depict the main character of your book more consistently. This allows you to establish a more familiar bond between the cover, chapter illustrations, and the story world.

Fortale does not limit the book experience to text. See the world of the story with cover and chapter illustrations; listen to your book with page narration, podcasts, and background audio. Switch between reading and listening as you wish; continue your story where you left off while travelling, resting, or whenever you want to share it.

Every book has its own path. In a tale, you can highlight imagination; in a story, you can deepen the choices of characters; in a workbook, you can explore concepts in a clear order. Fortale offers a flexible space to produce books in different genres suitable for different reading purposes from a single idea.

It also offers a powerful starting point for learning. When turning a topic into a workbook, you can choose your level; create a more structured learning flow with explanations, examples, reinforcement sections, quizzes, and related book recommendations. You can progress according to different narrative needs ranging from primary school to university level, and read or listen at your own pace.

Your books stay together in your personal library. Search, filter, and edit the works you have created; re-open suitable books, continue reading, and keep your access smoother thanks to the cache on your device. Prepare the books you like to be downloaded as PDF to read, print, or share; export as EPUB to use on your e-book reader.

Discover new stories in the Fortale community, bring the books you choose to publish to readers, like, and share. When looking for inspiration, examine works in different genres; grow your own library with books you love. You can return to the books you published yourself or added to your library directly from the reading flow.

You can create, read, and listen to books in Fortale with supported interface languages. Prepare a colourful adventure for children, a workbook for a subject you want to learn, or a new story for your own fictional universe.

Fortale is a flexible creative space for parents, students, teachers, language learners, and anyone who wants to write. Prepare a special tale for your child, create a topic explanation for your class, try a new fictional universe, or bring an idea in your mind closer to a book ready for publication. You set the direction in every book; Fortale accompanies you to bring your idea to life with text, visuals, and sound.

Write your idea. Build your world. Read, listen, download, and share your book. Let your next story begin in Fortale.
