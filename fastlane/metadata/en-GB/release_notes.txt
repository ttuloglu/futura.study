Fortale 1.0.5 makes your journey of turning ideas into books more fluid, clear, and reliable. The renewed welcome experience introduces tale, story, and workbook options more clearly with examples ranging from idea to completed book. Newcomers can grasp what they can create more quickly and start their own creation flows more easily.

Significant improvements have been made in book production, especially for workbooks. The approach of creating original and natural book titles that better reflect the content, rather than just repetitive or very general names, has been strengthened. The harmony between the title, short description, and chapter names is maintained more carefully; thus, learning-focused books are also prepared from the idea stage with a more tidy, readable, and purpose-fit structure.

Improvements have been continued in the tale and story production flow so that genre, theme, character, time, setting, and narrative prompts reflect more consistently in the result. The goal was for the bond between the cover, chapter illustrations, and the atmosphere in the text to be felt more naturally; and for characters, world, and plot to progress more balanced in long narratives. When you add an optional character portrait, this visual is used only as a creative reference to help consistently depict the main character of the relevant book.

The preparation and opening of books have been made more robust. Background preparation, downloading, and reading flow from the device cache in large content packages have been improved; situations informing the user while content is being prepared are presented more clearly. While the reading experience on mobile screens has been simplified, the balance between layout, text, and controls on wide screens has also been reviewed.

In the audio experience, the opening of the narration has been simplified; podcast production, voice selection, progress indicators, and downloadable audio flow have been revised for a more consistent experience. When you want to read your book, listen to it, or export it as PDF or EPUB, the status of operations is kept more visible. Thus, it becomes more fluid not only to turn an idea into text but to carry it to a book completed with its visuals and sound.

You can continue reading the works you published yourself or added to your library directly in the community feed. The read action on book cards takes you to the personal reading flow without the step of re-adding the work when appropriate. The experience of discovering, liking, sharing, and growing your library proceeds more seamlessly this way.

These improvements focus on small but important touchpoints along the entire path of a book from the first idea to a readable work. Understanding options during creation, opening the prepared book, following the audio version, and returning to your own library from the community now form a more understandable whole. The aim is for you to progress with fewer interruptions between the book's visual, text, sound, and access flow.

This version also renews Fortale's visual language and interaction details. A more integrated atmosphere was aimed for, from welcome screens to the book creation area, and from community pages to the reading view. 1.0.5 takes the experience of creating, reading, listening, and sharing further with stronger initial guidance, more careful book information, more reliable book access, improved audio flow, and more direct community reading.
