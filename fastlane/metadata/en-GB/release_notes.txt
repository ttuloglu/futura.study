Fortale 1.0.4 makes personal and exciting book production more powerful. The focus of this version is the portrait-supported production experience, which transforms the character of your dreams into a more familiar, consistent, and impressive narrative. With the optional portrait addition flow, you can include your main character in the book in a more visually stable way, helping the character appear more integrated in the cover, chapter visuals, and scene atmosphere while producing tales, stories, and novels. The portrait is a creative reference used to depict the book's main character; the goal is to create a more recognizable and personal book feel in every chapter. Book production quality has been significantly improved in this version. The selected genre, sub-genre, age group, narrative tone, and creative prompts are now reflected more consistently in the text. A warmer, curiosity-inducing, and child-appropriate flow in tales; clearer scene transitions, stronger character motivation, and more vivid atmosphere in stories; and longer-running plot, more balanced chapter structure, and more immersive tension in novels have been targeted. Thus, Fortale focuses on creating a book experience that is enjoyable to read and has higher excitement, rather than just producing text from a short idea. Visual integrity has also been strengthened. The production flow has been arranged so that the link between the cover image, chapter visuals, and character narrative is established more carefully. Especially in portrait-supported books, it is aimed to achieve a more natural harmony between the character's appearance, the story world, and the scene tone. Users' tale, story, and novel ideas approach a more ready-to-publish form with cover, chapter visuals, narrative rhythm, and exportable book output. Stability improvements have also been made in the production and reading experience. Processing large book packages has been made more reliable, local caching of cover and book content has been strengthened, and waiting and timeout issues that may occur during book openings have been reduced. While the reading flow on mobile screens is kept cleaner, text size and layout balance on iPads and wide screens have been improved. The general visual language has also been made more readable, modern, and more compatible with the application's new book studio feel. This version also maintains the gaps that protect user trust. The purpose of the image uploaded in the portrait-supported production flow is to help consistently depict the main character of the relevant book. Produced book images and book outputs can be saved, but the principle that the portrait reference is not used as a permanent profile image, advertising asset, or separate media archive is maintained. In general, 1.0.4 is a development version focusing on portrait-supported personalization, higher quality and more exciting book production, stronger cover and chapter visual integrity, more reliable book opening, and a more consistent reading experience. We continue to refine the core mechanics of Fortale to ensure that every story you craft feels unique, professional, and deeply engaging. By enhancing the way our AI interprets your creative vision, we have ensured that the nuances of your plot, the depth of your characters, and the aesthetic appeal of your book's visual elements work in perfect harmony.
